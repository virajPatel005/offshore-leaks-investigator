Help & examples

Offshore Investigator answers connection questions about the public ICIJ Offshore Leaks records. It follows recorded links (officer, intermediary, registered address) in a FalkorDB graph and shows the path behind every answer.

How to read an answer

Score — the mean rarity of the intermediate nodes on the path; a rarer stepping-stone scores higher. A direct link shows “not applicable: direct link, no intermediate nodes”.

Hub marker (double ring) — a node with more than 1,000 recorded neighbours. High degree makes a link less informative, not fake: hubs are excluded from ranked paths and named in the “excluded hubs” box when that changes the answer.

Dashed edge “(possible match)” — a soft link (similar, same_as …): the records are linked in the data, but the identity is NOT confirmed.

Question sets

Two public datasets are loaded together on the icij graph: Offshore Leaks and the Panama Papers. Cross-dataset questions follow “possible match” links between the two.
